- [ ] P0.2 — the named prediction cell (NUMERIC, falsifiable — written forward before any conversion): the roofline's own per-call number is the denominator. For the vocab matmul (640 × 14,989 fp16 ≈ 19.2 MB) on `AnePeaks::m5()`, the module's own formulas give `compute_ms ≈ 0.002`, dispatch floor 0.11, `memory_ms ≈ 0.34` → **`runtime_ms ≈ 0.34 ms`, and `runtime_ms` ALREADY models DRAM streaming of the weights — the prediction is about the EXCESS over `runtime_ms`, never over `compute_ms`** (dispatch alone is ~50× compute_ms; a ratio against compute_ms would be unfalsifiable). TWO postures, two predictions:
  - **Posture A — per-step host loop (the P2.4 default):** the joint call shape is tier-INVARIANT by construction (one encoder frame + decoder state in → vocab logits out, at every tier) → **prediction: per-call p50 is FLAT across tiers (±15 %) and ≥ the roofline's `runtime_ms`**; a measured p50 BELOW `runtime_ms` is a calibration row AGAINST `AnePeaks::m5()` (the bandwidth fit is documented conservative), recorded as such — not a contradiction. Falsifier: per-call p50 varying > 2× between `[56,0]` and `[56,13]` — that would mean something beyond call shape drives tier behaviour (CoreML re-planning, cache effects); investigate, never explain away. **A flat curve is the named outcome that favours dispatch amortization over a per-call working-set cliff** — it would reclassify FluidAudio's published tier collapse as a batching/amortization effect, not a per-call one. **Posture-A-only does NOT test the working-set hypothesis — if only Posture A runs, the bench record says the attribution is UNTESTED, never refuted.**
  - **Posture B — batched over the chunk's frames (a REQUIRED variant of P2.4 — it needs no B1 fusion; P2.7's fusion is the separate stretch):** the per-call shape GROWS with the tier, so the working-set question bites per-tier → **prediction: batched-call p50 ≥ 3× its own `runtime_ms` at the short tiers (collapse beyond the modeled DRAM streaming), falling monotonically to ≤ 1.5× by `[56,13]`**. Falsifying outcomes: < 1.5× at every tier (CoreML's tiling near-optimal → the FluidAudio working-set attribution REFUTED for this shape, recorded as such), or ≥ 3× flat across tiers (collapse without tier dependence).
  - FluidInference's "only fits the working-set at the 2 s tier" stays THEIR hypothesis throughout; the same cells discriminate it from dispatch amortization.
