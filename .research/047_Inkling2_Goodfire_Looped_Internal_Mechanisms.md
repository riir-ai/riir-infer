## TL;DR

Goodfire published (2026-11-20) the first internal-mechanism study of Inkling 2 Mini — the interpretability complement to Research 038's serving record: **how** the looped+attention-residual model actually routes information, **who** coordinates it, and **how to decode** it. Four findings with workspace reads: (1) the exact keep/gain message-gate closed form + the 104-reader structure — §1 pins 038 §1's loose "learned gate" description and confirms the DCA adoption (Research 691) at exact-weight level; (2) an **exact, modelless input-share decomposition** (per-layer contributions to any layer's input, shares summing to exactly 1, pure dot products) — the cheap flow monitor a looped checkpoint lane would want, signal-diffed against our interventional census (§2); (3) a weight-space **writer/reader "tagging" census** (3.5M MoE down_proj columns × 104 reader vectors) showing block-final layers (6/12/18) write FOR downstream block readers, plus a **massive outlier coordinate acting as a cross-loop routing control bit** (d_2027: RMS-normalized +32.4 ≈ 46% of the activation norm, +spike at block-3-final of loops 1–2, −cleanup spike at loop 3) — a NEW outlier-function class beyond the sink/verbosity taxonomy our lanes carry, and the 2-way block softmax reducing exactly to σ(gap), which the model drives to saturation through one outlier coordinate — an analogy to the house sigmoid gate shape, not a validation of it (the model still trains with softmax; §4) (§3–4); (4) the lens-decoding result — **bare logit-lens fails mid-block on looped models while early-loop layers read BETTER than mid-block ones (inverted vs vanilla transformers), and decoding through the final block ("coda-lens") restores legibility** — a pre-registered entry posture for any future looped-checkpoint run of readout_screen/census lanes, whose T5 remedy (J-lens) would be mis-targeted and whose T4 confirmation could misattribute lens-blind layers to the screen (§5). ## 1. The exact mechanics (what Goodfire's appendix pins beyond R038 §1)

Research 038 §1 described the loop design and "attention residuals + message gate" loosely from the release blog. The Goodfire appendix pins the exact forms (d = 2304, 24 layers, four 6-layer blocks, blocks 2–3 run T = 3 passes):

- **No cumulative residual stream.** Each block keeps its own running sum of its six layers' outputs (attention + MoE sublayers), from zero; token embeddings are "block 0", RMSNorm'd. v_i = Σ_{w∈B_i} o_w.
- **Depth-wise attention** (the AttnRes shape, Kimi Team cite): each sublayer reads a softmax mix over the completed block sums + the current running sum, with a learned per-sublayer query q: x = Σ_j α_j v̂_j, α_j = softmax(q·v̂_j/√d), v̂ = v/RMS(v). The appendix states its own breakdown — 31 attention-sublayer + 24 MoE-sublayer + 1 pre-unembed = **56 depth-wise readers** (the appendix's row, quoted as stated; it does not reduce to the 24-layer arithmetic, so we do not re-derive it).
- **Cross-loop attention** (loops t ≥ 2): each looped layer carries FOUR learned readers u_Q, u_K, u_V, u_MoE ∈ R^d, one per sublayer input; each reads a softmax mix over blocks 2 and 3 of the PREVIOUS loop. 12 looped layers × 4 = **48 cross-loop readers**. Total **104 readers**.
- **The keep/gain gate** (the exact closed form 038 §1 lacked): z = P[RMSNorm(x); RMSNorm(m)] with P ∈ R^{64×2d} (a 64-dim bottleneck), κ = 1 − tanh(Fz) ∈ (0, 2), γ = tanh(Gz) ∈ (−1, 1), F, G ∈ R^{d×64}; x' = κ⊙x + γ⊙m. The gate is per-coordinate (κ dampens/amplifies the current loop's input, γ adds or SUBTRACTS the message), not a scalar.
- **Output:** a final depth-wise mix over v_0..v_4, RMSNorm, unembedding.

**DCA adoption confirmed at the weight level** (feeds `../katgpt-rs/.research/691`): the u_Q/u_K/u_V three-way split of the cross-loop readers IS DCA's published QKV-split multiway axis (the Google-blog adoption claim, Research 691 §6), u_MoE is the MLP-side fourth reader, and the depth-wise reads over block sums are the AttnRes shape. One dated line added to 691's source list this session.

## 2. The exact input-share decomposition — the modelless flow instrument

Because every mix is a softmax over RMS-normalized block sums and the gate is elementwise, each layer's input decomposes EXACTLY: x' = Σ_w c_w with c_w = κ⊙α_{i(w)} r_{i(w)} o_w (current loop) or c_w = γ⊙β_{i(w)} r_{i(w)} o_w (previous loop), and the share s_w = c_w·x'/‖x'‖² satisfies **Σ_w s_w = 1 exactly**. Attributing per-layer outputs is pure dot products over block sums that ALREADY materialize in any forward — zero training, zero extra passes, one modelless instrument.

Findings (13,233 FineWeb tokens, 400 docs): depth-wise attention ≈ a sliding window (recent layers dominate); cross-loop reads go to **block 3** overwhelmingly, and block 3 reads ITSELF from the previous loop (redundant with its depth-wise access to block 2 — the model routes around double-reading).

**Signal-diffs (§3.6 discipline, run this session):**
- vs `../riir-infer/.issues/1241` (neuron census / katgpt-pruners `mech_attribution`): 1241 is OUTPUT-side INTERVENTIONAL attribution — dose-grid pushes over down_proj columns, KL observable, one forward per candidate (§2 of the 1241 lane: ~23 s/candidate mid-layer, the 557-CPU-h sweep arithmetic). The share read is INPUT-side, closed-form, zero extra forwards. Sibling instruments, different axis; the share read is the cheap monitor the interventional census cannot replace, and the census is the causal half the share read cannot prove.
- vs `../riir-infer/.issues/1250` (readout_screen): 1250 reads WEIGHTS ONLY (per-down_proj-column vocab readout) — no activations, no flow. The share read needs one forward's block sums but answers a question 1250 cannot ask (which layers feed which).
- **Workspace mapping:** lane intel only — no looped checkpoint is in model scope (owner law: Bonsai/ternary + qwen3.8; the looped lane rides 1221 at LOW priority). If a looped checkpoint ever loads in riir-infer (Dory GGUF lane), the share read is the first-pass flow monitor, and its softmax-mix algebra must be re-derived against OUR loader's forward — Dory is structurally different (re-injected recurrence vs attention residuals; the 1221 addendum records exactly this divergence class), so the sum-to-1 property is NOT assumed transferable.

## 3. Writer/reader "tagging" — the weight-space census and its screen sibling

Method: all 24 × 147,456 = 3,538,944 MoE down_proj column vectors ("writers") × 104 reader vectors, |cos| per pair (368M pairs), top 0.001% → threshold |cos| ≥ 0.30 → 3,490 pairs, broken down by writer layer × reader layer. Findings: block-FINAL layers (6, 12, 18) dominate the writing — layer 6 writes mostly for block 4's readers, layer 12 for blocks 3+4, layer 18 for block 4 and (heaviest of all, all of block 3 but especially its final layer) for the NEXT loop's block-3 entry readers (layer 13). Interpretation: late block layers **"tag"** activations so downstream/next-loop readers know which block to read — the weight-space dual of the observed read-from-block-3 dynamics.

**Screen sibling, zero owners (a gap for us — the method itself is owned by Goodfire, who ran exactly this census on their early-access weights):** this is structurally the readout_screen move (per-unit vector × target-vector alignment, weights-only, offline, once per checkpoint) with a DIFFERENT target family — readout_screen (1250) aligns every down_proj column with the **unembedding/vocab** rows ("what does this unit say"); the reader census aligns them with the **104 architecture reader vectors** ("which block does this unit tag"). Pinned claim: *reader-alignment census for looped checkpoints, consuming down_proj columns × reader vectors, distinguished from readout_screen by the target family and the looped-architecture consumer.* Pre-registered prediction, testable offline if a looped checkpoint ever loads: block-final layers carry the reader-aligned columns. Not filed — the consumer (a looped checkpoint in our lanes) does not exist; ungoat discipline.

## 4. The massive outlier as a cross-loop ROUTING CONTROL — a new outlier-function class

- **The datum:** coordinate d_2027 (of 2304) carries an average RMS-normalized value of **+32.4 — 46% of the activation's entire squared norm** in the looped blocks. It surges POSITIVE at layer 18 (block-3 final) in loops 1 and 2, and shows a large NEGATIVE spike at layer 18 of loop 3 — the model **cleans it up** when no future loop remains.
- **The function:** with two blocks, the cross-loop softmax reduces EXACTLY to β_3 = σ(s_3 − s_2), a sigmoid of the pairwise score gap; the gap decomposes coordinate-wise (gap = Σ_i u_i δ_i). At layer 13 (block-3 entry, loop 2), **d_2027 alone contributes 17/19/11/6% of the u_Q/V/K/MoE gap terms**; the top-5 outlier coordinates account for 60/62/32/30%. One saturated coordinate (plus four siblings) substantially PINS which block the next loop reads.
- **Analogy, not evidence (verdict round-1 correction):** σ(gap) is the exact algebra of any 2-way softmax — but the model is still TRAINED with softmax everywhere (this gate over 2 sources, the depth-wise mix over 5+), so this validates nothing about the house "sigmoid, never softmax" law. What the datum adds is a worked example of the mechanism shape: a learned sigmoid-shaped gate driven to saturation by ONE steered outlier coordinate — worth remembering when designing 2-way gates; no primitive claimed (the identity is trivial and published).
- **Outlier taxonomy extension:** our shipped lanes currently carry outliers as attention-sink class (the `sink_audit` lane — katgpt-rs Issue 981 closed `5ad264521`, its runtime follow-up riir-infer 1180 closed `80d7ca94` with the gemma2 ungated census Bench 953 on disk) and as weight-audit NORM signals (the `weight_audit` lane, landed `3dc77e23` — "norm features fire on natural massive-activation outliers, measured"; the issue record closed-removed `e034f0e9`, HISTORY carries the arc). d_2027 is a third class: **a cross-loop routing control channel with a lifecycle** (write at loop end, consume at next loop start, erase when looping ends). The weight_audit census detects the SHAPE and does not attribute function; the Goodfire gap decomposition is the function-attribution method (weights + cached activations, closed form).
- **Named, not filed — the interventional follow-up:** push d_2027-class coordinates and read the β flip. This maps EXACTLY onto Issue 1241's `census_column_scale` push shape (activation-column scaling, weights never move, thread-safe). Gated behind the 1241 sweep's outcome per its own lane law; a looped checkpoint is also out of model scope. Recorded here so the mapping is greppable.

## 5. Lens decoding for looped models — the actionable pre-registration

Evaluated on 1,250 FineWeb docs (tuned-lens trained on 23,750), four lenses:

| Lens | Result on Inkling 2 Mini |
|---|---|
| **logit-lens** (unembedding at intermediates) | FAILS except the final few layers per loop |
| **tuned-lens** (learned affine per site) | WORKS well |
| **J-lens** (averaged Jacobian to final state) | NOT well measured by top-50 metrics — open, per Goodfire's own footnote (rides the NDIF Looped Lens Challenge) |
| **coda-lens** (apply the ENTIRE final block — block 4, over the full context, since block 4 carries sequence attention) | WORKS well, no training |

The counterintuitive shape: in vanilla transformers logit-lens works mid-to-late; on the looped model **early-loop layers decode BETTER than mid-block layers across ALL loops**. 

**⚠ Naming-collision guard:** riir-infer's "J-share" (Issue 1250 T2, the NNLS verbalizability share that buckets units High/Middle/Low) is a DIFFERENT instrument from Goodfire's "J-lens" (Jacobian decode lens) — and from 1250 T5's planned "corpus-averaged J-lens directions" (arXiv:2607.15495), which IS the same Jacobian family as Goodfire's J-lens. Never grep-conflate the three.

**The pre-registration (lane intel, no NEW issue filed; a one-line pointer added under 1250's T5 row this session):** Goodfire's result splits by instrument, and the split matters:

- **Activation-based readouts** (the census lanes — 1241's KL observable, the 1237 `screen_auroc` tier): decode through the coda (apply block 4 / the final block over the site's context). A bare-unembedding logit lens misreads mid-block layers as garbage; the coda restores legibility. Cost: the corpus forward the site already runs PLUS one extra 6-layer block-4 pass per readout site — offline, never a serving path.
- **The weights-only screen** (1250 T1: down_proj columns through the unembedding + final norm, no context): coda-decoding is NOT applicable as-is — you cannot push a lone column vector through an attention block. The coda can only be APPROXIMATED (e.g., run only the coda's MLP/norm path per column, skipping sequence attention) — a DIFFERENT instrument that must be named as one if ever built, never presented as "coda-lens on the weights-only screen".

**T5/T4 reframe (the corrected danger, replacing this note's earlier "T5 false-fires" wording):** on a looped checkpoint, 1250 T5 firing would be a CORRECT diagnosis — its trigger is "middle-band readouts too weak rather than the screen failing outright", and the lens genuinely IS weak mid-block on looped models. The danger is the REMEDY: T5 prescribes J-lens directions (arXiv:2607.15495), and J-lens is UNPROVEN on looped architectures (Goodfire's metrics are an open question), while coda-decode is the measured-working decode — so on a looped checkpoint T5's remedy should be coda-decode first, with J-lens only after the looped-lens measurement question resolves. The misattribution risk sits UPSTREAM at T4 (screen-vs-census confirmation): layers that are lens-blind rather than screen-blind could be charged against the screen. Trigger: a looped checkpoint loading in any riir-infer lane (1221's lane owner; LOW priority per the model law — this line and the 1250 pointer exist so the mis-targeted-remedy class is greppable before it fires, not to open a lane).

## 7. Caveats

- Goodfire's corpora are small (13,233 tokens for flow, 1,250/23,750 docs for lenses); findings are labeled preliminary by the authors, and the J-lens measurement question is explicitly open (their footnoted metrics mismatch).
