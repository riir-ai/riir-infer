## TL;DR

2DSD accelerates **looped language models** (a shared transformer block applied R times per token — Ouro/Nanbeige/Dory) by speculating along **both axes at once**: tokens (a block-diffusion DFlash drafter, k=16) and loop depth (early readouts p^r1/p^r2 draft and fork before the final loop), unified by the parameter triple (r1, r2, k) — "It recovers standard SD when drafting occurs only after the last loop and depth-axis SD when the block size is one." Verification is lossless for greedy AND sampling via ordered cascade rejection sampling with residual chaining (paper Eq 5), and last-loop KV sharing with a lagged window (W=64) removes the R× per-loop KV read traffic, adapted by a cheap two-pass self-distillation where needed (3.4–6.7 h on 4×H100 per model; Dory1.0-4B/nGPT needed none). Geomean 4.70×/5.73×/7.64× greedy vs native AR (vs DFlash 3.50×/4.52×/6.21×).

**Distilled for riir-infer (records only; nothing lands):** (1) the **drafter DATA recipe** — train the head on the TARGET's own rollouts against the target's argmax, with in-flight-context randomization and repetition truncation — is the trigger-(3)-shaped recipe record for the ranked-above Bonsai-target specdec lane (riir-train Issue 702, PARKED, stop line pre-registered); (2) the **two-pass detached-reference law** (frozen where inference reads approximated state, live where autoregressive, reference recomputed per microbatch) is a shelf training recipe for future lossy serving arms; (3) the **verifier-design deltas** (ordered cascade over HETEROGENEOUS per-candidate proposals; sampling-lossless fork/prune) are lane intel for the paused lanes — the substrate already ships the residual pair (`katgpt_core::speculative::sampling`, Leviathan Eq 3) and greedy-side tree verify (`gdn2/tree_verify_bridge`, T43); (4) corpus verdict MARGINAL C+ (riir-refine snapshot row): one thin Triton transcription candidate, three positive-instance kills.

## 1. What 2DSD is (as shipped, quotes at the pin)

**Looped LM + the two axes.** A looped LM refines token hidden states by applying a shared module f_θ of L layers for R loops, with per-loop KV caches C_t^(r). An early readout at loop r maps h_t^(r) through the LM head to a distribution p^r — every loop depth is a free draft-quality predictor of the final p^R. Depth-axis SD (LoopSpec, arXiv:2609.17184 — the cited base) exploits only that axis; DFlash-class block drafting exploits only the token axis; 2DSD unifies both.

**The four stages (paper Fig 2, R=4, r1=1, r2=2, k=4 illustrative; shipped k=16):** (a) block drafting after prefill — the drafter proposes k−1 future tokens in one pass; (b) early review at r1 — drafts reviewed in order by rejection sampling against p^r1 with its recorded proposal distribution q_d, "At the first rejection, we draw a replacement token a from the normalized residual" (α(y) = min{1, p^r1(y)/q_d(y)}; a ~ p^r1 ⊖ q_d); (c) gated fork at r2 — "The gate opens when the most likely token z_j under p^r2 differs from y_j"; a fork samples its anchor with "Pr(a = v) ∝ [p^r2(v) − p^r1(v)]_+", or a previously-rejected draft whose token equals z_j is PROMOTED with its suffix re-reviewed against p^r2 (the rejected pool is a free candidate cache — drafter work is re-priced, never discarded); (d) commit + prune at R — "candidates are verified using the preceding token's last-loop distribution, committing accepted tokens and pruning alternative branches."

**Losslessness (Eq 5).** Candidates i = 1..m carry recorded proposals Q_i; verification starts P_0 = p^R and on rejection updates P_i = P_{i−1} ⊖ Q_i; acceptance at i is min{1, P_{i−1}(y)/Q_i(y)}. The paper proves "the entire cascade starting at candidate i follows P_{i−1}" — combining acceptance at i, acceptance at a later candidate, and residual resampling after all rejections yields exactly P_{i−1}(v) — so the output follows P_0 = p^R at every committed prefix, for greedy (argmax matching) and sampling alike. **Q_i is unconstrained** — non-factorized (block-diffusion) proposals and multiple heterogeneous draft sources compose losslessly under the one verifier. That is the generalization beyond Leviathan-style single-proposal verification.

**Last-loop KV sharing (Eq 6).** "a query at position t and loop r reads the KV cache entry at position j according to: C^(R)_j, j ≤ t − W; C^(r)_j, t − W < j ≤ t" — committed history = ONE shared last-loop bank; per-loop caches retained only inside the lagged window W=64 covering unfinished tokens. Motivation is measured: "speedup over AR falls from 2.38× at 2K context to 1.28× at 32K, although acceptance rates remain nearly constant" — the decay is KV read traffic, not acceptance. The approximation boundary is auditable: Fig 10's cross-loop key-cache cosine-similarity map drives an exception set N = {(R,ℓ)} ∪ {(1,1)} ("We therefore retain the native KV cache for this layer in both training and inference") — the first-loop first-layer mismatch is a MEASURED allowlist row, not a hand-tuned hack. Dory1.0-4B "uses the base checkpoint without adaptation" (nGPT-based robustness).

**Two-pass self-distillation (App C; the training recipe that makes sharing survivable).** From `training/sharekv/rule.py` (byte-quoted at the pin): the shared rule + `kd_policy` = `{"prompt": "native-one-pass", "generated_history": "last-loop-shared-outside-W", "train_window": 64, "window_includes_self": true, "first_reader_native": true, "last_loop_current_causal": true, "student_pass1": "native-no-grad-recomputed-every-microbatch", "student_pass2": "shared-with-native-prompt-grad", "only_approximation": "old-response-final-KV-from-student-P1"}`. Pass 1 = the student's own native no-grad forward (recomputed every microbatch — a stale reference would inject checkpoint-drift noise), only the detached last-loop KV kept as the frozen reference; Pass 2 = response queries read the detached Pass-1 last-loop KV outside the window (the only approximation) and their OWN Pass-2 entries inside it — "Using current-pass KV cache in the last loop allows gradients to flow through earlier tokens' last-loop KV cache outside the local window." Settings: one epoch / 128M tokens (OpenThoughts3), packed 32,768 document-isolated, AdamW (0.9, 0.95) lr 1e-5 peak / 5% warmup / cosine → 1e-6; cost "3.4, 6.7, and 4.0 hours ... on four H100 GPUs" for Ouro-1.4B/2.6B/Nanbeige-3B; quality deltas ≤ ~1 pt across GSM8K/MATH-500/HumanEval+/MBPP+.

**Depth-conditioned DFlash drafter (App D.4 — the DATA recipe).** One drafter serves ALL loop depths: 5 layers, target hidden width, frozen target embeddings + LM head, features from 5 target layers per loop, learned mask embedding, block k=16. Training depth sampled per block: "sample r ∈ {1, 2, 3, 4} with probabilities (0.5, 0.125, 0.125, 0.25)"; "For r < R, we also randomly vary the number of in-flight tokens in the drafter context to cover different scheduling situations"; targets = the target's final argmax over target rollouts (13K prompts, OpenThoughts3/UltraChat/CodeAlpaca, 16K budget); "We stop rollout generation when repetition is detected and retain the prefix before repetition"; official DFlash/Speculators recipe, "six epochs with block size k = 16 using AdamW with a learning rate of 6 × 10−4".

**Results (their tables; foreign silicon H100/H200-class + foreign models — never headroom for our rows).** Greedy geomean: Ouro-1.4B 4.70×, Ouro-2.6B 5.73×, Dory 7.64× vs native AR; vs DFlash 3.50×/4.52×/6.21×; vs LoopSpec 2.65×/2.95×/2.81×. Nanbeige (R=2, early-only) 2.37×. Ablations: sharing recovers the long-context decay; the last-loop choice beats first-loop sharing; acceptance stays ~constant with context.

3. **Verifier-design deltas for the paused specdec lanes** — the ordered cascade over heterogeneous proposals (Q_i unconstrained, residual-chained, Eq 5) and sampling-lossless fork/prune verification. Coverage: the residual PAIR ships (`katgpt_core::speculative::sampling::sample_residual_distribution_into` — "Residual distribution sampling into pre-allocated scratch buffer (zero-alloc)", Leviathan Eq 3, ported riir-engine Plan 008) and greedy-side TREE verify ships (`katgpt-attn/src/gdn2/tree_verify_bridge.rs` + `tree_forward.rs` — GdnTreeVerifier, commit-on-accepted-leaf, "This is the **only** state mutation in the tree-verify workflow", T43 chain/branching parity tests, module docs carry an exact-vs-approximate posture). The ordered heterogeneous cascade + sampling-lossless forks do NOT ship — recorded here as the verifier design datum for whichever lane un-pauses first. **No katgpt-core filing: no consumer lane exists (substrate-first law — a consumer-less cascade primitive is a parallel system).** The TV closed forms (E[acceptance] = 1 − TV(Q,P); residual mass = TV(P,Q); fork mass = TV(p^r1, p^r2)) are textbook identities worth one line in the lane design when it opens: schedule selection (r1, r2, k, W) becomes an offline corpus-measurement problem.

**NOT transferable (each with its reason):** early loop-r readout drafting — GDN is time-recurrent; there is no per-token depth readout to draft from. Last-loop KV sharing — GDN has no per-loop KV; the recurrent state IS the recurrence (the loop-free adjacent surfaces are R023 FlashStates state-prefix-cache + R006 bekko shared-prefix KV, already recorded). The (1,1)-layer exception and W=64 — model-family-specific hyperparameters. Their absolute tok/s — foreign silicon + foreign models, never headroom for our league rows.
