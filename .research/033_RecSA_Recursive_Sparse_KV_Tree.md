## TL;DR

RecSA builds a **fixed-fan-in positional tree over the KV cache** (16 tokens → node, 16 nodes → node, top level ≤4K nodes; 4 levels at 2M) and searches it **top-down with fixed k**: the query selects 256 candidates per level (the node containing it + the top-255 scored), their ≤4K children become the next level's candidates. Two moves no shipped arm here carries: **(1) candidate-local RoPE** — every candidate (selected OR summary) takes its index in that level's candidate list as its RoPE position, the query the last index, so every relative distance stays <4K at ANY context length (the extrapolation lever: same tree with standard RoPE scores 1.0 RULER at 131K; with YaRN 1.9 at 524K; candidate-local keeps 60.5 at 1M); **(2) summaries in the softmax** — unselected nodes are not dropped, each enters the same softmax as one pooled entry, so every token is covered by exactly one entry and no sink/local window is needed (the self-node is always selected, so exact local context is free). Node keys come from a **distilled pooler** (~25K params/layer, shared across KV heads AND levels; mean + gated pull toward top-scoring children + MLP correction) trained by KL against the frozen dense model's per-level subtree attention mass — with a **shift penalty** because an MLP shared-shift rotates differently per candidate under candidate-local RoPE and becomes a positional-bias shortcut. Two training stages, all at 32K (Stage 1: distill pooler, frozen backbone, 64 selections/level so 3 levels exist at 32K; Stage 2: backbone under RecSA, LM loss, 256/level), **0.62B tokens total**. Result: RULER **60.5 @1M / 49.3 @2M** vs 40.0/27.6 for the best identically-trained baseline (Dense+DCA); matches Dense at 32K (94.7); PG-19 ppl flat 10.9–11.1 from 131K–1M; short-context within 2 pts of Qwen3-4B. Wall-clock on one H200: prefill 3.2× / decode 3.8× at 524K; decode ~6 ms FLAT from 131K–524K (vs dense 9→23 ms), ~8 ms at 1M with host-pinned exact tokens (<7% of cache on GPU, gather ≤600 MiB/step). DSA+DCA keeps distances bounded too, same token budget, yet −24 pts at 1M → **bounded distances necessary, not sufficient; the coverage-guaranteeing tree + summaries-in-softmax is the difference**.

## 1. Paper core findings

1. **O(log L) selection AND reads.** MoBA-class block-sparse scores every block (O(L)); RecSA scores ≤4K candidates per level, and a query at 2M reads ~12K entries (1.16% of context at 1M, 0.60% at 2M — token budget 5,888 @32K → 11,990 @2M, matched across all sparse baselines).
2. **Summaries in the softmax (coverage guarantee).** Unselected nodes enter as one (pooler-key, mean-value) entry each; every token is covered by exactly one entry; approximate mass still counts in the normalization. The self-node is selected at every level → the tokens right before the query are always exact, no attention sink or sliding window.
3. **Candidate-local RoPE is THE extrapolation lever.** Distances bounded by candidate-list width (≤4K) at any length. Ablation (same training, same tree): standard RoPE → RULER 1.0 at 131K; YaRN → 1.9 at 524K/1M; candidate-local → 60.5 at 1M. This is a hierarchical generalization of ReRoPE's window-bounded positions (ReRoPE = flat; candidate-local = per-level, order-preserving, gaps removed).
4. **The distilled pooler fixes mean-pooling's known failure mode.** Mean pooling waters down a subtree holding one strong match among weak ones (their Figure 5 — the same "a block summary can hide a single relevant key" LSA names, App. E.6). Pooler = mean + gated pull toward top-scoring children along a learned direction + small MLP correction; ~25K params/layer, shared across KV heads and ALL levels (the level-4 tree at 2M reuses stage-1 params distilled on 3 levels at 32K).
5. **Shift penalty — the reindexed-position training guard.** An MLP shift shared by all keys of a level rotates differently per candidate under candidate-local RoPE → a score depending on candidate RANK, not subtree content; the KL loss can descend through this positional bias alone. Penalty: penalize the per-level shared shift, leave inter-node differences free. Without it the MLP barely helps; with it, removing the MLP hurts more at longer contexts. **Novel to the workspace record — generalizes to ANY trainer that reindexes positions.**
6. **Training data efficiency, controlled.** 0.62B tokens all at 32K (synthetic retrieval + docQA + books + code + instructions) vs HiLS's 50B CPT for its 512× extrapolation (katgpt-rs 399) — **~81× less** for a controlled-baseline ≥1M result; baselines (Dense/MoBA/DSA backbones) trained on the same data, same recipe, then evaluated with YaRN and DCA wrappers.
7. **Fixed k suffices at ≥1M — mass-adaptivity is not necessary.** 256/level, constant, beats the strongest baseline by 3 pts at 131K growing to ~20 at 1M. This answers an open axis R021 left: ZHA's top-p mass-adaptive routing vs fixed budgets — at ≥131K with trained summaries, fixed-k wins the controlled comparison. (Retention-rule axis stays unresolved in general — top-p unfixed here, just unnecessary for this operating point.)
8. **Kernel co-design that transfers.** Prefill: RoPE rotation split (key side rotates by slot-independent 0..15, query side carries the slot-dependent part — 16 adjacent queries of a thread block share every KV block; each bottom-level block serves 4–8 of 16 queries). Decode: last-node-per-level incremental update; 16-segment occupancy split + merge + top-256; **ONE CUDA graph over all 36 layers** — legal because the ≤4K candidate cap gives every kernel a fixed launch grid; KV offload (exact tokens host-pinned, upper levels <7% of cache on GPU, selection stays all-GPU, gather ≤600 MiB/step, GPU suffix cache). Decode stays ~6 ms at 131K–524K with >half the step outside attention.

### 2.2 §3.6 signal-diffs on the coverage dismissals

- **vs `pyramid_topk` (R595, the shipped tree):** Bench 612's negative tested the UNTRAINED mean-pool arm at 32K+ iso-quality on a frozen model — below the knee, original positions, route-only (unselected mass discarded). The modelless pyramid arm's negative stands unchanged. Not covered.
- **vs ZHA (R021, closest cousin):** complementary evidence, one contradiction. ZHA's evidence stops at 256K and its summaries keep original positions (the HiP-class limitation RecSA's blog names explicitly); RecSA's evidence reaches 2M with reindexed positions. Uncovered deltas: candidate-local RoPE (nothing in ZHA carries re-indexing), the extrapolation headline, kernel co-design.
- **vs the 020 tree-degrades law:** the law's evidence splits in two, and RecSA speaks to only one half. (a) The **positional tree** — LSA's Block-Sparse (Tree), 020 finding 2, ~63 RULER at 1M with ORIGINAL positions — plus our HGA negative: RecSA (positional tree, REINDEXED positions, trained backbone, 60.5 @1M) **refines this half**: the ≥1M degradation axis for positional trees is distance exposure, removed by candidate-local re-indexing (the paper's own ablation: same tree, standard RoPE → 1.0 at 131K; candidate-local → 60.5 at 1M). (b) The **content-clustered tree** — D-LSA (Tree), 020 finding 3, built in the RoPE-free index space (020 finding 7), still loses past 1M on GLM — is **NOT explained by this axis**: its index has no distance exposure; at most the final attention step's original positions carry it (unmeasured — an open hypothesis, never claimed). - **vs the flashmemory budget arm (bench 1214, NOT-promoted):** the arm's top-ρ selection scores ALL KV blocks per tick (`GqaFlashMemorySelector::select` + `FmTopRhoSelectMetal`) — the O(L)-selection shape RecSA exists to kill. **4th re-open lever: tree-indexed selection** (O(log L) scoring + fixed-grid capture). Honest caveat: 1214's loss decomposition (scoring vs gather vs kernel overhead) is unmeasured; RecSA's decode breakdown (the whole tree search fits inside a 6 ms step alongside a PCIe gather at 131K–524K) is the design argument, not a Metal proof. Recorded, not filed — the trigger stays the lane's own re-open conditions plus a decomposition showing selection dominates the 0.38× wall.
- **vs HiLS (katgpt-rs 399):** the trained-extrapolation cousin (512× extrapolation, 50B CPT; hierarchical softmax factorization = summaries-in-softmax published prior). ## 4. §4 published prior-art landscape (no novelty claimed)

- **Flat window-bounded positions:** ReRoPE (bojone/rerope — blog-cited as the comparison prior; candidate-local is the hierarchical, order-preserving generalization). YaRN / DCA (2402.17463) = context-extension wrappers — delay, do not remove, the distance failure (RecSA's own ablation).
- **O(L) trained selection:** MoBA (2502.13189), DSA (2512.02556) — score every block/token; RecSA's Table-1 contrast is exactly this.
- **O(log L) selection, original positions:** HiP (2406.09827), InfiniteHiP (2502.08910) — hierarchical cost, but "keep each key's original position and still need context extension" (blog §1). Training-free baselines (InfLLM, InfiniteHiP) degrade monotonically in RecSA's table.
- **Trained hierarchical trees:** ZHA (riir-infer 021, arXiv:2610.42112, 2026-10-30 — top-p, trained reference queries, ≤256K evidence); Twilight (2502.02770), Double-P (2602.05191), SpotAttention (2606.22874) — the R021 §4 sweep (2026-11-03) covers the top-p family; HiLS (2607.02980) the trained landmark extrapolation family. None carries per-level candidate re-indexing.
- **Flat content-cluster:** LSA (riir-infer 020, arXiv:2610.42147) — crossover law + RoPE-free index space; its tree arm degrades past 1M WITH original positions.
- **Memory-tier hierarchies (different genus):** SPIN (2604.26837), HiSparse, HieraSparse, SGLang HAS — GPU/host storage tiers for sparse-attention serving, not per-query tree search over the cache; adjacent, not competitors on the algorithm axis.

## 5. Honest caveats

- **Wall numbers are H200/Triton/CUDA.** Our boxes are Metal (m3/m5ultra, bmo) + the 4090 — the crossover and the 6 ms decode figure must be re-calibrated per box before any band is quoted (the R021 caveat applies verbatim).
- **Trained-model requirement.** Candidate-local RoPE + the pooler are NOT retrofitable onto a frozen GGUF checkpoint without stage-2-style training; the zero-shot arm (untrained tree) is exactly the class our three negatives measured at ≤256K. Nothing here moves any serving default.
- **League-model shape.** Qwen3-4B is dense (36 attention layers); our league Bonsai-2-27B is GDN-hybrid (~16 attention layers) — the cost model scales differently per attention-layer share, and the GDN layers need no tree at all.
- **Short-context cost.** Prefill is slightly SLOWER at 32K (1.6 s vs 1.5 s dense) — the fixed-k + summary overhead at in-distribution lengths costs a little; our league shapes sit exactly there (another knee-law instance).
- **Far from solved.** RULER 49.3 at 2M; PG-19 ppl flat but PaperTrail-class tier-3 torture (020 finding 8) untested by RecSA.
- **Sources.** Abstract + blog read directly (Table 2, ablation, kernel sections); paper App-level training hyperparameters NOT read. **The two recipe deltas exported to riir-train 666 (per-level subtree-mass KL target, shift penalty) are blog-§2.3-sourced — a contradiction found when the appendix is read requires a 666 correction commit.** Advocacy estimates are unmeasured rows, never quoted as numbers.
