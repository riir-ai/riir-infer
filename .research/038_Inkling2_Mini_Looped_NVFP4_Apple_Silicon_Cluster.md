## TL;DR

Inkling 2 Mini (Thinking Machines, 2026-11-20, open weights) is the strongest signal yet that **looped transformers + native 4-bit deployment are a production serving posture, not a research curiosity**: 26.4B-total/3.4B-active MoE, 24 layers with the middle 12 run three times (48 layer passes/token, 5.4B weight activations per token), trained AND deployed at NVFP4 (20 layers) + MXFP8 (last 4) with "no conversion loss", ~20 GB resident — and it beats our league-model family (Qwen3.8-27B: DeepSWE 48.2% vs 42.2%, SWE-bench Pro v2 Hard 77.9% vs 73.8%) while running on laptops. Three independent Apple-Silicon implementations shipped within days of each other (HF MPS Metal path in PR #51062, Locals' MLX-nvfp4 build, maya-inkling-mini). For us: (1) it is the **second real looped checkpoint family** (after Dory, issue 1221) with the varloop variant + the first published loop-depth monitorability data; (2) it is **NVFP4 lane evidence for the dense qwen3.8 decode row** — plausible but expensive, ranked below the named levers; (3) it is **NOT** a Bonsai lever (ternary PQ2_0 at ~2.2 bpw is denser than NVFP4's ~4.5 bpw — never). PR #51062 itself: corpus-MARGINAL (positive instances of B379 faces + our own DQ posture), lane-intel real.

## 1. The model (blog, compressed)

- **Looping design:** 24 layers in four 6-layer blocks; blocks 2–3 run 3×. Middle-looping beat last-looping on 27/28 evals (compute-equivalent gain 1.51–1.91× vs 1.24–1.36× at matched loss, step-2000 estimate). The unpruned non-looped twin cost 7.5% less training compute but needed **140% more inference compute** to match the looped model's loss on 512k-token agentic traces — looping is an inference-compute lever, bought at training time.
- **Attention residuals + message gate** (Kimi Team 2026 cite): each layer reads earlier block outputs and learns its own mix; passes 2–3 of the middle blocks additionally receive the previous pass's middle-block outputs ("the message") through a learned gate that starts fully closed (loop-until-close = the non-looped model) and opens over training — the stability trick that lets looping be introduced mid-training.
- **Native low-bit:** NVFP4 = E2M1 payload + one E4M3 scale per 16 + FP32 per-tensor scale; MXFP8 = E4M3 + one E8M0 per 32. Expert layers quantized NVFP4 on every forward from pretraining through RL (GB300-accelerated); deployment precision = training precision.
- **Pruning + healing:** trained at 48 sparsity (8 of 384 experts), pruned to the 128 most-used per layer midtraining, healed on 1T distilled tokens, then SFT + RL (2.2T tokens, 2000 steps) with on-policy distillation for alignment.
- **Benchmarks (record, never a bar — different harnesses):** DeepSWE v1.1 48.2% (Qwen3.8-27B 42.2%), SWE-bench Pro v2 Hard 77.9% (73.8%), Vals Index 46.5%, PMPP-Hard AUC 455.8 (262.5 — the kernel-optimization bench, notable given our league), BrowseComp 79.0%, AA-Omniscience in line with size (looping ≠ knowledge), Stanford intel-per-joule 0.703 on DGX Spark (vs 0.430 Qwen3.6-35B-A3B; theirs at MXFP8, others NVFP4/INT4). Weaknesses: multi-turn chat / many-constraint instructions (MultiChallenge 50.0%, ComplexConstraints 33.4% — behind Qwen3.8-27B).
- **Varloop research checkpoint** (`TML-d24-varloop-research`, 1–5 passes trained, RL at 3): the loop-depth experimental axis — see §4.

## 3. Lane verdicts

**3.1 NVFP4 in riir-infer — "do we need it?"** Answered with grep evidence this session: **zero FP4-quant surface in riir-infer** (every `float4` hit is the CUDA vec4-f32 type in attention/deltanet kernels; no nvfp4/mxfp4/e2m1 anywhere). Verdict by lane:
- **Bonsai-2-27B (league priority): NEVER as a WEIGHT format.** PQ2_0 ternary at ~2.2 bpw is denser than NVFP4 (~4.5 bpw effective); NVFP4 weights are a bandwidth LOSS there. Activation-side FP4 (the W4A4 prefill path PR #51062 also demonstrates) is a separate axis from ternary weights and is NOT assessed here. Owner model-scope law (2026-10-26) scopes production to Bonsai/ternary + qwen3.8 anyway.
- **qwen3.8 dense (league second): MAYBE, not next.** The decode row is bandwidth-governed (the Issue-1211 family law: "only load-shape changes move it") — a weight-format change IS a load-shape change, and ~8.5→~4.5 bpw would roughly halve weight bytes on the tg128 0.894× row. But the cost is a full lane: loader (GGUF has no NVFP4 type today — the T0 is a llama.cpp/Unsloth FP4-type census), Metal+CUDA dequant/GEMV kernels, two-level scale handling, and the lossy-surface promotion bar (per-family conditional retention, league GOAT cell — never bit-identity). The named levers stand: f16-B graduated (Issue 1225), ffn_gate_up 28.1% next. NVFP4 is a plausible follow-on lever, pre-registered HERE as the T0 gate: **an NVFP4 dense lane opens only if (a) a GGUF FP4 type exists to ingest (or we mint our own, the cq3 precedent), (b) the league cell clears the retention bar per family, (c) it beats the ffn_gate_up lever's wall-clock price at matched quality.**
- **What we DO own already:** the corpus playbook (B335 mxfp4→INT8 recipe: whole-matrix unpack pass gated by rows/tile, spread-constrained scale fold, counting-sort route permute) + the B379 E2M1 dual-LUT decode face — the Metal decode half of an NVFP4 lane is largely transcribed decision content awaiting a consumer. Note the honest caveat: B335's exact-INT8 scale-fold does NOT transfer to NVFP4 (E4M3 scales are non-pow2) — NVFP4 wants LUT→fp16 fused GEMV (the f16-B serving-twin precedent, Issue 1225).

**3.2 Looped-model lane — issue 1221 is the sibling, now with its second family.** Issue 1221 (Dory GGUF looped lane) owns the lane: loader + elastic k + last-loop KV. Inkling 2 Mini + TML-d24-varloop-research are the **second real looped checkpoint family**, and the richer one: a varloop variant whose loop count is a runtime dial, plus the first published loop-depth measurements (§4). No new issue filed — 1221 carries the lane; this note is the evidence row, and the T0-extension QUESTIONS are routed INTO 1221 as a dated addendum row this session (filing is not routing). The 1221 T0 tripwire should extend to: does the Inkling release (GGUF or NVFP4 safetensors) fit 1221's loader shape, and does the varloop config surface (`loop_num_passes`) map to 1221's elastic-k halter seam (the T3(b) `GainCostLoopHalter` cost_floor story is exactly the varloop dial's calibration instrument)?

**3.3 Specdec — trained looped drafters now exist off-the-shelf.** Locals ships a 0.64 GB draft model for a looped 26B (37→65 tok/s thinking); maya's tip carries a specdec-drafter arm (B379 T0). Our looped-specdec method record is 034 (2DSD); the paused 1179/1183 verdict stands unchanged (a Bonsai-target specdec lane ranks above restarting qwen3.8). Recorded, no filing — the pause re-open triggers are unchanged. 2026-11-24: the 2DSD family added a PUBLIC depth-conditioned drafter for the variable-loop Inkling itself — `minseo25/2dsd-inkling-2-mini-varloop-dspark-drafter` (DSpark-class: DFlash backbone + Markov head + confidence head, k=8; H100 NVL geomean 3.37× vs AR@R=3, beating the checkpoint's bundled MTP 2.77×; full record: R034 §5).

## 4. The monitorability data (why this note is more than a serving record)

The varloop study is the first published loop-depth axis, and every finding has an inference-side or decision-side read:

- **No-CoT time horizon** (Think Fast, 50% reliability, no CoT): 16 s @1 pass → 37 s @3, flat to 10 passes (10-pass execution works though RL was at 3 — loop-depth generalization is real). Read: looping moves compute from generated tokens into per-token internal depth — the loop count is a **quality/latency dial at serve time**, exactly the elastic-k seam issue 1221 already owns for Dory.
- **Serial reasoning scales past training depth:** N-Hop Lookup accuracy improves out to 10 loops (trained 1–5).
- **CoT compresses but stays legible:** on the GPQA-Diamond correct-set, mean reasoning tokens 3703 @1 pass → 1661 @5 (2.2× shorter), words-per-sentence and dictionary fraction unchanged, uncertainty markers 9.7→3.6 — the shorter CoT is "more direct, less backtracking", not encoded. This is the empirical answer to the "looped models hide reasoning" concern **at 26B scale**; the authors flag it may not hold at RL-trained higher depths.
- **CoT controllability stays poor regardless of depth** (≤0.1% instruction-following on CoT-Control-QA at 750–1250 tokens vs Qwen3.8-27B's 1.3%) and **LinuxArena stealth shows no clear trend with passes** (1.0–5.0% across 1–5).
- Decision-side read for the Reflex/Rethink family: a loop-count dial is a per-request **compute-for-accuracy knob** of the same shape as our escalation ladder (cheap→think), except the compute goes to internal depth instead of a bigger model. If a looped model ever seats in the decision family (3.4), the varloop axis is the natural ESC-rho surface. Recorded as lane intel only.
- **2026-11-26 addendum (Research 047):** Goodfire published the first internal-mechanism study of this model (loop decomposition, writer/reader tagging, the d_2027 outlier control channel, coda-lens decoding). The exact keep/gain gate closed form THIS note §1 describes loosely ("a learned gate that starts fully closed") is pinned there: z = P[RMSNorm(x); RMSNorm(m)] (64-dim bottleneck), κ = 1 − tanh(Fz) ∈ (0,2), γ = tanh(Gz) ∈ (−1,1), x' = κ⊙x + γ⊙m, with 104 reader vectors (56 depth-wise + 48 cross-loop, u_Q/u_K/u_V/u_MoE per looped layer). Full record: `047_Inkling2_Goodfire_Looped_Internal_Mechanisms.md`.
