## Why

vLLM + SGLang — both perf-rematch opponents — measurably collapse on concurrent agentic hybrid workloads (vLLM at 48 sessions on GLM-5.3-Flash: p90 TTFT 20.4 s, 24.7K median uncached tokens/req; FlashStates 2.36 s / 1.03K). **Our single-stream serving half already ships**: `riir-infer-gpu/src/qwen38_prefix_cache.rs` (Issue 742 T4) — whole-prefix KV+GDN checkpoints at caller-chosen anchors, restore + suffix re-fill, `note_write` lineage pruning, CUDA-graph-safe. What FlashStates adds that we lack, on that same lane: (1) **multi-session** reuse (742 T4 is explicitly single-stream; its own header names the successor — `katgpt_kv::radix_prefix::RadixPrefixTree` + `PagedKVCache` chunk-page seam, opt-in `radix_prefix_cache`, katgpt-rs Issue 771 / Bench 762: 2.45× hit-rate at equal page budget); (2) **cost-aware retention** — 742 T4 evicts by recency (+ the lineage prune, which is the single-stream dependency-miss rule), with no cost-density priority and no leaf-last; (3) **in-chunk kernel export**. The paper's ablation is the scoping evidence: ASC placement alone with LRU is NEGATIVE at 32 sessions (−14% vs vLLM) — retention is load-bearing; both halves ship together or neither is honest.
