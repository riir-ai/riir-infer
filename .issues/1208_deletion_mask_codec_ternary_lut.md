## Background

Deletion Tuning deploys a trained BINARY mask over a frozen model's gate projections — zero new weight values, <0.05% of weights deleted, 1 bit/weight, 0.5–30 MB bit-packed+zstd vs GB-scale FFT checkpoints. The deployment is GD-free (frozen weights + deterministic mask), which makes the entire serving half a substrate question for THIS repo: a mask artifact format, its application to dense GGUF weights and to packed ternary PQ2_0 codes, per-task hot-swap, and audit tooling. The authors released trained masks (e.g. `grpo/qwen2.5-7b-simplerl-zoo`), so every cell below is runnable TODAY with zero training.

**Ternary-native angle (the differentiator):** deletion (→0) is in-distribution for PQ2_0 — code 1 IS the zero symbol; codes are {0→−1, 1→0, 2→+1, 3→+2} and the spare code-3 state is measured UNUSED (0.000% across 30.72M real Bonsai-27B weights, `src/quant/q2_0.rs` module doc). A masked PQ2_0 model stays a valid PQ2_0 model; the kernel bit-path is unchanged by construction. The trained-mask content producer is riir-train Plan 494; closed-form content solvers are T7 here.
