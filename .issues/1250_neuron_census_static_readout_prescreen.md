# Issue 1250: Static readout + verbalizability pre-screen for the neuron census
**Status:** Open — unclaimed (katgpt-rs research 693, 2026-11-25; lane: riir-infer 1241 census)

Anthropic Fellows pair (Part 1, alignment.anthropic.com/2026/neuron-basis-theory/) reads every MLP neuron's output vector w (down-proj column) through a lens and shows readouts match activation behavior far above chance (73%/31%/19% at high/middle/low J-share vs 4–6% controls), that type-3 polysemanticity (two unrelated concepts under ONE sign) is ≤~1%, and that low-J-share neurons instead align with later attention queries. Our census lane (1241, `neuron_census_gguf.rs`) sweeps candidates interventionally (~557 CPU-h for the full Bonsai-27B sweep) with a layer-band prior but NO weights-only pre-screen; the cheapest existing screen (`census_screen::screen_auroc`, katgpt-pruners 1237) still consumes activations — forward passes on every unit. A static readout screen is a strictly cheaper tier in front of both, and the type-2 finding (each SIGN of a neuron is its own concept unit) exposes a gap: `CensusRow.direction_sign` keeps only the winning arm's sign, flattening two-concept neurons to one row.

Caveat carried from research 693: the pair's statistics are dense-SwiGLU 8–32B; transfer to our ternary GDN checkpoints is measured by T4, never assumed. Offline lane only — the 666 lens serving-demotion binds serving claims, never offline census artifacts (684's posture line). 388's J-lens REFUTED verdict was a serving-prefilter latency refutation, not an offline per-model construction one.

- [ ] T1 — readout map v0: logit-lens readout (unembedding rows over final-layer norm) of every down_proj column, sign-split (+w and −w as separate units), on the local `riir-train/data/Qwen3.5-0.8B-Base-Q8_0.gguf`; top-k tokens per unit; deterministic, BLAKE3-digested artifact in the `sink_audit`/`weight_audit` lane shape. The artifact reports the high/middle/low bucket split PER LAYER BAND (early/middle/late) — without this, T4 cannot distinguish "the screen fails" from "the logit lens is too weak at middle layers" (the T5 trigger; the pair's own data shows logit-lens readouts weakening before late layers).
- [ ] T2 — verbalizability fraction (J-share shape): fraction of each unit vector explained by a ≤25-token nonnegative combination of lens directions (NNLS or projected gradient; Issue-940 sequential fold order; deterministic tie rule), percentile-ranked against same-layer random directions; the artifact carries the bucket (high/middle/low) per unit, with the same per-layer-band rollup as T1.
- [ ] T3 — both-signs characterization, threshold PINNED: the screen carries per-sign concepts so a type-2 neuron (mascara +w / radio −w) is two units; the census-side `CensusRow` extension to per-sign concepts happens iff ≥10% of screened units have both signs reading distinct high-bucket concepts — below that bar, record the finding in the artifact and leave the row format alone.
- [ ] T4 — ordering-prior wire + GOAT, bars PINNED:
  - **Leg 1 (efficiency):** budget unit = forward passes of the census driver. Screen-ordered sweep vs the unordered (layer-band-only) sweep at the SAME candidate population, with the screen's one-time construction cost charged to the screen arm (never amortized away). Pass = the screen arm reaches the same interventional confirmation count with ≥30% fewer forward passes.
  - **Leg 2 (transfer):** the top-decile screen units confirm at ≥2× the random-order confirmation rate, bootstrap lower 95% bound above 1.0×, over ≥30 interventional confirmations on the same checkpoint. Fewer than 30 confirmations → UNDECIDED, never PASS.
  - Either leg failing where it ran closes NEGATIVE — including one leg failing while the other is UNDECIDED (one clear failure decides; UNDECIDED elsewhere never rescues) — and the screen demotes with the lane (shared kill bar with 1241, mirroring `census_screen`'s docstring discipline).
- [ ] T5 — upgrade path (deferred): corpus-averaged J-lens directions (Gurnee et al. 2026, arXiv:2607.15495) replacing logit-lens v0; triggered specifically when T1/T2's per-band report shows middle-band readouts too weak rather than the screen failing outright; one per-model offline construction, never a serving path.
